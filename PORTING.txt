ARIENTEWORLD - FORGE 1.20.1 PORT HANDOFF
Updated: 2026-10-30

STATUS AND SCOPE
================
Buildable development port with active Ariente terrain, surface/underground
cities, dungeon complexes, transport networks, vegetation, ores, natural spawns
and portals. This is NOT a complete gameplay port or a release candidate. Exact
original terrain/tree-shape parity, authoring tools and full gameplay validation
remain unfinished. Builds and focused tests are not proof of full parity.

This file owns world generation, authored structures, city AI/security/loot,
spawning, entrance/arrival generation, world commands and editing tools.
../Ariente/PORTING.txt owns equipment, machines, resource processing, recipe/JEI
work, vehicle implementations, rendering, shared HoloGui and packaging details.
Read BOTH files when continuing. In particular, ore PLACEMENT works here while
ore DROPS/SMELTING and several armor effects are still missing in Ariente.

IMPLEMENTED WORLD GENERATION AND BEHAVIOR
========================================
Dimension and terrain
- Dimension ID: arienteworld:ariente. Data-driven dimension/biomes use the custom
  ArienteChunkGenerator and ArienteBiomeSource, not the earlier temporary plain
  terrain checkpoint. Do not replace this with generic terrain again.
- Original marble terrain, floating-island noise, six biome profiles, seven
  seeded biome zoom layers, grass/foliage colors and bubble/sphere/glow-bubble/
  spike landmarks are active. Modern quart sampling replaces the old final
  Voronoi layer; seed-for-seed identity with 1.12 has not been established.
- Purple sky and fog, original sun/moon/stars and no vanilla clouds are restored
  through ArienteDimensionEffects. Companion blocks, models, translations,
  creative entries and basic loot are registered.

Cities, districts, stations and structure assembly
- All eight original complex plans generate: city0_0, city0_1, city1_1, city1_2,
  city2_1, city3_1, city4_1 and city5_1. These include surface, underground and
  floating complexes and the large nine-by-nine layout.
- Separate sprawling landscape cities are also active: towers, parks, roads,
  walls, covered underground streets and local levitator routes. These are the
  large surface/underground districts requested from ariente_world2.jpg.
- Complex placement retains the original sixteen-chunk grid and seeded plan/name
  selection. CityFeature writes only its current chunk; nearby chunks are not
  force-loaded just to generate a city. City/terrain queries are tested for
  seed/order isolation and city generation in shuffled chunk order.
- Parts retain authored cellars, stacks, variants, palettes, facades, spawners,
  machine settings and security metadata. LegacyBlockStates translates original
  IDs/colors/metadata/slabs. Blocks and machine settings rotate/mirror together.
  Palette cells are TWO characters; do not parse authored rows as one-char cells.
- Surface footprints have black-marble foundations and original distance/noise
  terrain transitions. Underground complexes retain their original height;
  landscape district level is Y=65. Intercity tunnels/station infrastructure is
  around Y=30; station travel beams are at Y=32. Preserve these conventions.
- Intercity routes, nine-chunk station layouts, elevator shafts and local beams
  generate. City AI can dispatch soldier levitators along loaded routes. Route
  queries do not load neighboring chunks. Vehicle mechanics live in Ariente.
- PaneConnections restores authored glass/pane neighbors, including chunk-border
  connections. Wide door markers use opening width before transforms. Single
  ship markers infer paired jambs at panel height, correcting the floating ship
  interior doors. RenderingRegressionTests covers the actual c4_1_e part under
  all eight transforms; panel orientation follows the repaired marker.

City AI, equipment and alerts
- CityAISystem/CityAI persist and initialize machine/security data, storage loot,
  key/channel IDs, generator fuel, guards/sentinels, alerts and reinforcement
  waves. Initialization/ticking waits for the required footprint to be loaded;
  standing near one partially loaded building need not initialize an entire city.
- Sentinels detect visible survival players within roughly 50 blocks, report
  sightings and refresh alerts. Creative/spectator players and a working Scramble
  helmet upgrade are ignored. Combat drones/guards also report their targets.
- Ship forcefields activate during alerts, not simply because a player is near
  a peaceful ship. Generated-ship tests verify detection, obstruction/range,
  city notification and powered shield activation through authored equipment.
- Sentinels are ALERT-ONLY in original master. Combat drones do the shooting;
  their ranged goal, laser renderer and collision fixes are in Ariente. Do not
  turn sentinels into combat drones just to reproduce a mistaken recollection.
- Existing city storage/security setup is active; this does not certify every
  loot output, resource drop, crafting path or combat balance decision.

Population and travel
- Seven tree varieties use native configured/placed features with the original
  materials, weighted selection and biome densities. Black bushes, dark grass
  and small flowers are active. Tree algorithms adapt the original shapes; they
  are not bit-for-bit copies of the old generators.
- Trees require valid soil under every trunk root, including all four roots of
  giant trees. They must not turn authored city floors into dirt.
- Marble deposits and all nine ore types use the original vein ranges, attempts
  and Y ranges. Ores run before city construction. Resource loot and processing
  are separate Ariente tasks and remain incomplete.
- City biomes use the original soldier/master weights and group sizes; other
  biomes use overworld hostile creatures. Soldiers use hostile mob caps, normal
  ground/darkness checks and weapon equipment. Long-running spawn balance is
  not proven by checking spawn tables and a short generation fixture.
- Underground Overworld entrance rooms use the configured entrance chance.
  Pearls locate nearby seeded intact entrance warpers rather than coordinates
  of cities in another dimension. Broken entrances are skipped; the pearl uses
  the original nineteen-chunk search radius. Ariente owns pearl item/rendering.
- Arrival buildings use the original sixteen-chunk grid. Portals remember the
  entrance for return travel, then use a valid Overworld bed or spawn fallback.
  Arrival warpers have return charges and charge counts synchronize to clients.
  Missing arrival structures have a fallback placement path; that existing path
  is not a request to add further old-world migration support.

CONFIRMED REMAINING WORK AND WHY
================================
Generation controls and parity
- config/WorldgenConfiguration.CITY_DUNGEON_CHANCE (cityDungeonChance, default
  0.7) is declared but not read by active complex selection. The setting was
  missed when the generator was restored. Wire it consistently into generation
  AND search/API lookup so commands/portals do not disagree with placement.
  The separate Overworld entrance chance (default 0.002) IS used.
- Compare original terrain landmarks, district layouts and tree/vegetation shapes
  against master/reference images. Native tree implementations and quart biome
  sampling intentionally replaced obsolete APIs; exact parity remains unverified.
  Preserve deterministic/chunk-local behavior while correcting actual omissions.
  Do not label all vegetation/ores/terrain as unported: their active replacements
  already exist. Full seed identity is not an established completion criterion.

City authoring and administrative tools
- EditMode and EditModeClient remain archived, with related commands:
  CommandEditMode, CommandSaveCity, CommandSyncChunk, CommandVariantCreate,
  CommandVariantGet/Set/List/Switch, CommandInfo, CommandPacify and CommandCityCard.
  Only exploration/search commands were migrated; the authoring workflow was
  deferred while restoring runtime generation.
- Restore the relevant command suite using Brigadier, current client/server
  networking and the active part/palette/variant schema. Compare master as well
  as intermediate legacy files; do not copy obsolete APIs or activate the whole
  legacy source tree. Verify save/export/import, synchronized previews, security
  cards and variant edits against the current structure loader.

World integration still requiring validation
- Complete fresh-world progression: encounter an entrance, locate it with a
  pearl, charge/travel/return, obtain city resources/blueprints/cards, fight
  guards, use captured technology and explore the large districts.
  Ariente currently blocks that route with missing ore drops/smelting, original
  mob resource loot and some equipment effects. Those fixes belong in its repo.
- Validate AI across large/partially loaded footprints, unload/reload, alerts,
  waves, key drops/access, generator depletion and machine/security persistence.
- Exercise natural spawning for sustained periods and check caps, difficulty,
  density and equipment instead of assuming table definitions prove balance.
- Travel long station/intercity/local routes with passengers and check corners,
  elevators, chunk transitions, AI transport and multiple players. Short cart
  tests do not certify a complete transport system.
- Multiplayer portal destinations, per-player return points, reconnects, safe
  arrival, charge consumption and interaction with different bed/spawn states
  need full play testing beyond the existing round-trip fixtures.
- Perform a full server datagen/resource audit. Active world data is currently
  authored directly; a complete successful datagen run has not been certified.
- Standalone dependency publication/wiring and final versions/releases remain
  unfinished. version=1.12-0.0.31-alpha is a historical label, not the target MC

BUILDING, RUNNING AND REPRODUCING CHECKS
======================================
Run from /data/Projects/McTech20 with Java 17 (see Ariente's handoff for the local
JAVA_HOME example; do not add machine-specific paths to build configuration):
  ./gradlew :Ariente:build :ArienteWorld:build :ModHoloGui:build
  ./gradlew :ArienteWorld:runGameTestServer -Pariente_runs
  ./gradlew :ArienteWorld:runClient -Pariente_runs
  ./gradlew :workspace:runClient
  ./gradlew :ArienteWorld:tasks --all
Use the workspace directory or --project-dir ..; merely running ../gradlew from
a mod directory selects its standalone settings and may fail on old fallback
coordinates. Preserve standalone support, but do not replace local dependencies
with published jars just to make the workspace launch.

The -Pariente_runs property enables this project's focused development runs.
They load local ArienteWorld/Ariente/HoloGui/McJtyLib/TOP source outputs and filter
local built jars from launch classpaths so Forge cannot prefer stale jars.
GameTest output is under build/gametest; test sources are not in release jars.
The combined workspace client additionally has Embeddium 0.3.31 / Oculus 1.8.0;
the focused client does not automatically include them. Test the actual renderer
stack relevant to a reported bug as well as native graphics when appropriate.

Optional client fixtures are NOT enabled by the normal runClient command.
To reproduce, include the gameTest source set in the client run and pass JVM
properties to the GAME JVM, not just -D flags to Gradle. A small external init
script can configure it without changing tracked build files, for example:

  gradle.afterProject { p ->
      if (p.name == 'ArienteWorld') {
          p.minecraft.runs.client.mods.arienteworld.source(p.sourceSets.gameTest)
          p.tasks.withType(JavaExec).configureEach {
              if (name == 'runClient') {
                  dependsOn p.tasks.named('gameTestClasses')
                  jvmArgs '-Dariente.armorGuiCheck=true',
                          '-Dariente.hologramRenderCheck=true'
                  doFirst {
                      p.file('build/visual-check').mkdirs()
                      workingDir p.file('build/visual-check')
                  }
              }
          }
      }
  }

Launch with -I /path/to/that-init.gradle -Pariente_runs. Supply an isolated test
save under build/visual-check/saves; the init script does not create a save.
RenderingRegressionTests builds the shared fixture in arienteworld:ariente around
X/Z=90016..90027, floor Y=240. Run the dedicated tests to regenerate it, then copy
that STOPPED test world into the isolated client's saves, or copy the fixture
region into a compatible isolated test save. With commands enabled, teleport to
90020.5 242 90023.5 in the Ariente dimension. Do not modify a running world's
region files or overwrite the user's workspace/run worlds.

Compatible armor/hologram drivers prepare their own gear/GUI once the fixture
is loaded. Other optional game-JVM properties are ariente.machineGuiCheck,
ariente.cartRenderCheck and ariente.pearlRenderCheck. Use the relevant driver;
independent drivers can move the player or overwrite each other's fixtures.
TextureModelChecks runs automatically when its fixture and source set are loaded.
Previously used /tmp/ariente-hologui-client.gradle added the workspace's exact
Embeddium/Oculus runtime dependencies; /tmp/ariente-hologui-vanilla-client.gradle
selected Fabulous in the isolated options. Treat these scripts as conveniences,
not prerequisites; recreate from the tracked tests and the setup above if absent.
Confirm the real datagen task before running it and use isolated output first.
Previous successful datagen was CLIENT-ONLY for Ariente models through a focused
launch, not proof of a complete companion/server data-generation run.

FOUR-PHASE COMPLETION PLAN
==========================
Phase 1 - Finish world controls and identify real generation omissions.
  Connect cityDungeonChance consistently, compare active terrain/district/tree
  features with master and reference images, and correct verified omissions
  without adding old-world migration. Keep Ariente's immediate armor fixes in
  its repository; do not treat broken equipment as broken dimension generation.
  Exit: generation, find commands and API placement predicates agree, and actual
  parity differences are documented rather than inferred from legacy filenames.

  Verify city loot/cards, sustained spawning/alerts and entrance/arrival/return
  behavior while Ariente restores resource drops, processing and recipes.
  Exit: a fresh survival player can reach cities, obtain meaningful resources
  and security access, use captured/crafted equipment and return safely.

Phase 3 - Restore authoring tools and complete city/transport workflows.
  Port edit/save/variant/sync/admin commands against active assets; exercise large
  city AI, security, stations and long routes, coordinating Ariente vehicle and
  machine repairs. Keep optional new cube/boss content separate from parity.
  Exit: authored content can be edited/exported/reloaded and runtime workflows
  work across chunk boundaries and save/load, with targeted checks for repairs.

Phase 4 - Integrate, validate and prepare release.
  Complete multiplayer, long-running world/AI/spawn/portal tests, datagen and
  asset coverage, standalone/workspace dependency checks and release metadata.
  Exit: both handoffs and the gameplay guide reflect tested behavior and any

  playthrough and integration evidence, not compilation alone.
