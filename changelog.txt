1.20-0.0.31-alpha:
- Replaced obsolete Autofeed module blueprints in city loot with Energy Holder blueprints for the shared equipment power supply.
- Vanilla mobs no longer spawn naturally in Ariente biomes outside cities.
- Soldiers and guards can carry two-handed Energy Lances and fire lasers. Lances and their blueprints are rare finds in storage cubes.
- Advanced bases have stronger foot soldiers and combat drones whose lasers can kill players in unenchanted netherite armor with one hit, even on Easy.
- Soldiers and occasional master soldiers now spawn naturally inside every dungeon type, including underground and huge dungeons, at light levels of 11 or lower.
- Expanded the huge cube dungeon with varied multi-deck interiors, valuable storage-cube loot, and a large arena reserved for a future boss.
- Huge cube dungeons now generate half as often, with their loot cubes recessed into framed walls.
- Doubled surface city buildings and parks, with taller technological spires.
- Added procedural underground machinery islands and alternate detailing for ships, stations, and smaller dungeons.
- Added an ArienteWorld chapter to the Ariente Tablet, covering expeditions, cities and their defenses.
- Ported to Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).
- Alerted dungeons deploy mobile soldier reinforcements independently of their fixed defenders and neighboring troops.
