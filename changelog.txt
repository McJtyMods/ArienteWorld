1.20-0.0.31-alpha:
- Scout patrols now require a surviving AI core at their launch base and newly chosen destinations. Flights already heading to a captured base finish their journey and retire on arrival. Patrol frequency and traffic limits are unchanged.
- Scouts now patrol much closer to the terrain and bank visibly through turns. Defeated pilots leave ships to descend for recovery instead of removing them, and release their base for replacement patrols.
- Scout patrols launch much more frequently, with rapid first encounters, 10–20 second repeat opportunities and bases considered up to 896 blocks away from a flying player. Existing default settings and old cooldowns upgrade automatically; the two-scout limit remains in effect.
- Large surface and floating bases occasionally launch soldier-piloted scout ships on inter-base or circular patrols. Scouts make hull-aimed laser attack runs against visible ground or flying players, search their last sighting for one minute after losing sight, and resume their route once the pursuit ends. Solid cover stops fire, nearby patrols are limited to two, and excess ships divert. Launch frequency is server configurable.
- Added rare Darkbats to the wilderness: larger flying predators that dive at players for heavy melee damage, in daylight or darkness. They do not spawn in cities or dungeons. Natural spawns are limited to three nearby Darkbats per player by default, configurable with darkbatMaxPerPlayer.
- Sabre-wielding soldiers now kill unarmored players in one hit on every non-Peaceful difficulty; master soldiers hit harder.
- Some city buildings, floating dungeon rooms, huge cube control desks and underground console banks now have alien glyph displays. Salvage their unreadable Alien Text Modules by recovering them from the display or breaking its base; they have no blueprint or crafting recipe.
- Replaced obsolete Autofeed module blueprints in city loot with Energy Holder blueprints for the shared equipment power supply.
- Vanilla mobs no longer spawn naturally in Ariente biomes outside cities.
- Soldiers and guards can carry two-handed Energy Lances and fire lasers. Lances and their blueprints are rare finds in storage cubes.
- Advanced bases have stronger foot soldiers and combat drones whose lasers can kill players in unenchanted netherite armor with one hit, even on Easy.
- Soldiers and occasional master soldiers now spawn naturally inside every dungeon type, including underground and huge dungeons, at light levels of 11 or lower.
- Expanded the huge cube dungeon with varied multi-deck interiors, valuable storage-cube loot, and a large arena reserved for a future boss.
- Huge cube dungeons now generate half as often, with their loot cubes recessed into framed walls.
- Doubled surface city buildings and parks, with taller technological spires.
- Added procedural underground machinery islands and alternate detailing for ships, stations, and smaller dungeons.
- Added an ArienteWorld chapter to the Ariente Tablet, covering expeditions, cities and their defenses.
- Ported to Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).
- Alerted dungeons deploy mobile soldier reinforcements independently of their fixed defenders and neighboring troops.
